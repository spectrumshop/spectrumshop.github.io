We find it first.

Spectrum is a UK online shop for trend-led everyday products. We pick a small number of things that solve something mildly annoying, and we sell them properly.

What we sell

Our catalogue is deliberately small — a handful of products at any one time, rotated as we find better ones. We stock practical, everyday physical goods: desk and home organisation, small accessories, and problem-solving gadgets.

We do not sell food, drink, supplements, cosmetics, clothing, or any age-restricted product.

How we sell

Products are shipped directly from our supply partners' UK and EU warehouses. We show products in real use, in short video, rather than in studio photography — because that is how you can actually tell whether something is any good.

Where to find us

We sell through our own online store and through TikTok Shop. Short product videos published to our TikTok account are produced by us and show our own listed products.
