3. Our legal bases

  • Contract — processing your order and delivering it to you.
  • Legal obligation — keeping transaction records for UK tax purposes.
  • Consent — marketing emails and non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — preventing fraud and improving our store, balanced against your rights.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, and we do not sell your personal data to anyone. Recipients are:

  • Our e-commerce platform, which hosts the store and processes orders.
  • Payment providers, who handle card payments directly.
  • Supply and fulfilment partners, who receive your delivery details so they can ship your order.
  • TikTok, where you buy through TikTok Shop — TikTok's own privacy policy also applies to that purchase.
  • Email and analytics providers, where you have consented.

Some providers operate outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred abroad, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

Order and transaction records are kept for six years, as required by UK tax law. Marketing contact details are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for up to 26 months.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your data; to object to or restrict processing; and to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, email [ADD YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within one month.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your basket and session working — these cannot be switched off. With your consent we also use analytics and marketing cookies to understand how the store is used. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings; the store will still work, but some features may not.

8. Children

Our store is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes affecting your rights will be notified by email where we hold your address.
